## Photography

Atmospheric destination imagery is served from Unsplash's image CDN in responsive, compressed sizes. Photography uses the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). Image URLs and source photo identifiers are listed in `src/worlds.js`. Original photo records can be found by searching the unique photo identifier on Unsplash. Images are illustrative; they do not establish the identity, inventory, architecture, hotel, dining facilities, or availability of any named destination.

## Typography and icons

DM Sans and Playfair Display are bundled through Fontsource (SIL Open Font License). Lucide icons use the ISC License. QR codes are generated locally with qrcode.react (ISC).

## Brands and concepts

This independent QPASS prototype is not an official website of any venue, club, hotel, operator, or brand. Vodafone Stadium, Al Ahly Store, and El Qalaa El Hamraa sections are non-bookable visual concepts, with no official logo assets or claim of affiliation. Giza Zoo prices, inventory, availability, and experiences are fictional local demo data. QR passes are not valid for admission.
